# Watermark Lite trial and paid editions

[中文对照](TRIAL-VS-FULL.zh-CN.md) · [User guide](README.md) · [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md)

Watermark Lite adds visible text and logo watermarks to still images on Windows 10/11 x64. Use the separate itch.io trial to check your own images before buying, or choose Microsoft Store's independently licensed offer.

## What the itch.io trial lets you check

The trial begins on first launch and continues while the application is closed. You can work with up to 3 images at once, choose from the 16 built-in templates, preview your settings and export during those 24 hours.

Trial exports do not add an extra Demo mark. Text or logos you choose as your own watermarks remain in the exported image.

After the trial expires, editing and preview remain available, but new exports require the separately purchased full ZIP. The trial does not automatically become the full package.

The full ZIP has no trial expiry. Its 1,000-image folder/drag import limit is not a promise that every computer can process 1,000 images reliably. Practical batch size and processing time depend on image dimensions, formats and available resources.

## Microsoft Store uses its own trial and license

The Store's published package offers a 1-day trial through Microsoft licensing. Its eligibility, entitlement and expiry are separate from the itch.io local trial. The itch.io 3-image cap and local timer do not define the Store offer.

Check the Store page and the native Microsoft Store application for the offer available to your account and region. Do not use the itch.io trial instructions to judge Store license status.

## Why the version numbers differ

The paid itch.io ZIP is 2.0.0, the itch.io trial is 2.0.3, and the Store package is 2.0.2.0. These numbers track the separate distributions and their release and licensing revisions. They do not establish feature parity, missing functionality in the full ZIP, or future upgrade rights.

A one-time purchase is not a promise of lifetime upgrades, unlimited-device use or a new commercial-use entitlement. Check the relevant product page for purchase terms and ask before buying if your intended use needs clarification.

## Local processing and internet access

Images are processed on your computer without cloud image uploads. Downloading and purchasing require internet access. Local image processing does not establish offline Microsoft Store authorization; offline Store licensing has not been verified.
